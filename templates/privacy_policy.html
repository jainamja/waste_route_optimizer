Privacy Policy

TrashTrekyx Driver & Fleet Management • Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how the TrashTrekyx Driver application collects, uses, and protects your information, specifically focusing on location services and driver tracking.

Summary: TrashTrekyx collects background location data exclusively to enable real-time route progress tracking, dynamic stop sequencing, and fleet navigation while an active shift is in progress.

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable route navigation and waste collection verification, the app collects:

  • Precise Location Data: GPS coordinates, heading, speed, and hardware satellite timestamps collected in both the foreground and background while tracking is active.
  • Authentication Data: Driver credentials and vehicle assignment tokens.
  • Operation Metrics: Stop completions, skips, bag delivery confirmations, and customer note logs.

2. Use of Background Location

The app requires continuous background location access to:

  • Provide turn-by-turn guidance even when the screen is locked or another navigation tool is active.
  • Automatically verify arrival at collection points.
  • Enable dispatcher fleet visibility on the live tracking dashboard.
  • Queue and synchronize route completion telemetry in cellular dead zones.

3. Data Retention and Security

Location data is transmitted securely via TLS encryption to our server and Firebase Realtime Database. Offline coordinates are durably recorded in an encrypted device queue and purged once synchronized.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please contact your fleet operations administrator.
