Privacy Policy for OnRoute Driver

Last Updated: October 12, 2026 · Application: OnRoute (com.wasteroute.driver)

This Privacy Policy describes how OnRoute ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information obtained from drivers and authorized fleet personnel using the OnRoute Driver mobile application and related fleet management services.

Prominent Notice on Location Data Collection

OnRoute Driver collects real-time location data (including foreground and background location) to enable live fleet tracking, route progress monitoring, and turn-by-turn waste collection dispatch by your administrator, even when the application is closed or not in use during an active driver shift.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location Information: Precise GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) captured periodically while a driver is logged into an active route.
  • Device & Authentication Information: Assigned driver credentials, assigned truck identifiers, device hardware state, and application performance metrics.
  • Route Operations Data: Timestamps of stops reached, customer confirmations, skipped stops, and collection completions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display real-time truck locations on the central dispatch management map.
  • To dynamically optimize collection routes and rebalance assigned stops based on current traffic and vehicle proximity.
  • To verify stop completion and delivery of municipal/commercial waste collection services.

3. Background Location Tracking Disclosure

Because drivers frequently switch to navigation applications (such as Google Maps) or lock their mobile device screens while driving between collection locations, OnRoute Driver requires continuous background location access. Tracking begins when a driver starts an assigned shift and automatically pauses when the shift ends or the driver logs out.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or location data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers. Location data is securely transmitted solely to:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database: For encrypted, real-time synchronization with authorized administrative dashboards.
  • Google Maps Platform: For navigation and reverse geocoding services.

5. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the OnRoute Driver mobile app and our backend servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption. GPS tracking data is retained only for operational reporting, route verification, and audit purposes.

6. Your Rights & Control

Drivers may stop location tracking at any time by ending their shift, logging out of the application, or revoking location permissions in Android device settings. Please note that active location permission is required for fleet assignment and dispatch operations.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or data practices, please contact your fleet administration or email us at support@onroute.app.
